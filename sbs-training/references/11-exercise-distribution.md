# Exercise Distribution by Frequency

Source: SBS Program Bundle by Greg Nuckols

## How Standard vs LF Differs

| Aspect | Standard | LF (Lower Frequency) |
|--------|----------|----------------------|
| Design philosophy | Full-body — mixes squat, press, and hinge on the same day | Movement-pattern grouping — consolidates similar movements on the same day |
| Available frequencies | 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x | 3x, 4x, 5xa, 5xb, 6x |
| 5x variants | Single tab | Two variants (5xa and 5xb) with different distributions |
| Per-muscle frequency | Higher — each muscle group trained on more separate days | Lower — fewer days per muscle group, but more volume per session for that group |
| Example (Strength 3x) | Day 1: Squat + Sumo DL + Incline (3 patterns) | Day 1: Squat + Deadlift + Front Squat (all lower body) |

**Core principle**: Standard spreads muscle groups across more sessions at lower per-session volume. LF concentrates similar movements for higher per-session volume at fewer weekly exposures per muscle group.

## Shared Layouts

- **All 3 strength standard variants** (Set Threshold, Last Set RIR, RTF) use **identical** exercise distributions. Only the autoregulation method differs.
- **All 3 strength LF variants** also share **identical** distributions.
- Hypertrophy Standard has its own distribution (different default exercises from strength).
- Hypertrophy LF has its own distribution.
- Novice LP and Novice Hypertrophy each have unique distributions.

This means there are **6 unique distribution patterns** for the 12 programs.

## Upper Back Exercises

**Intermediate programs** (Strength, Hypertrophy, all variants): Upper back exercises are NOT assigned to specific training days. They are configured in Quick Setup as a dropdown (Barbell rows, DB rows, Chest-supported rows, T-bar rows, Pull-ups, Chin-ups, Neutral grip pull-ups, Pull-downs). The user self-manages placement as accessories — minimum 2–3x/week.

**Novice LP**: Upper back exercises (Pull-downs, Barbell rows, DB rows) are programmed directly into the day layout, not left to user discretion.

**Novice Hypertrophy**: Back exercises (t-bar row, reverse grip pull-down, seated cable row, neutral grip pull-up) are fully programmed into each day.

## Per-Muscle Frequency Summary

Quick reference: days per week that include at least one exercise for each muscle group.

**Muscle group definitions**:
- **Quads**: Squat, Front Squat, Paused Squat, Leg Press, Hack Squat, Split Squats
- **Chest**: Bench Press, Close Grip Bench, Incline Press, DB Bench, DB Incline Press, DB Flyes
- **Shoulders**: OHP, Push Press, DB OHP, Machine Shoulder Press, Seated DB Shoulder Press, side/rear delt raises
- **Post. Chain**: Deadlift, Sumo Deadlift, Block Pulls, Romanian Deadlift, Glute-Ham Raise

| Program | Freq | Quads | Chest | Shoulders | Post. Chain |
|---------|------|-------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| **Standard** (Str/Hyp) | 2x | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| | 3x | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| | 4x | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| | 5x | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| | 6x | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| **LF** (Str/Hyp) | 3x | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| | 4x | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| | 5xa | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| | 5xb | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| | 6x | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| **Novice LP** | 3x–6x | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| **Novice Hyp** | 3x | 1 | 2 | 3 | 2 |
| | 4x | 2 | 2 | 4 | 2 |
| | 5x | 2 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

**Key takeaways**:

- Standard templates reach 3x quad and 3x chest frequency at 3+ days/week, then plateau — more training days reduce per-session volume without increasing per-muscle frequency.
- LF templates maintain 2x per-muscle frequency across most tabs. The exception is LF 5xb, which acts as a hybrid (3x quads and chest).
- Shoulders and posterior chain stay at 2x across all intermediate programs and frequencies.
- Novice LP always trains upper back 3x/week (programmed, not user-managed).
- Novice Hypertrophy has the most variable per-muscle frequency across its tabs due to its body-part split design.
