# Ch 4: SRA (Stimulus-Recovery-Adaptation)
Book: Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training | Authors: Israetel, Hoffmann, Davis, Feather

## Core Principle
Links Overload + Fatigue Management. Train → Rest → Repeat. SRA dictates when to train again.

## The SRA Curve
After effective training:
1. Stimulus triggers growth + fatigue
2. Recovery period (fatigue dissipates, growth occurs)
3. Adaptation complete → train again

Growth elevated ~1-3 days post-training. Fatigue can linger longer.

## Volume Landmarks & SRA
- MV: minimal recovery, zero growth
- MEV: small growth, minimal recovery
- MAV: best absolute growth
- MAV-MRV: most growth but recovery competes for resources
- Above MRV: recovery overwhelms growth; potential muscle loss

## Training Frequency

### Key Finding
Per-week volume is king. 2-4x/week per muscle group produces similar results when volume equated.

### Functional Overreaching
Higher per-session volume + longer rest = similar gains. Broadens acceptable frequency range.

## Problems with Low Frequency

### Fatigue > Growth Time Course
- Growth: ~1-3 days
- Fatigue recovery: up to 1 week+
- Low frequency = wasted non-growing recovery time

### Junk Volume
- Systemic fatigue accumulates across sets
- After ~10+ sets, motor unit recruitment degrades
- Later sets: high fatigue, low stimulus

### Technique & MMC Degradation
- Fatigue degrades form
- Mind-muscle connection fades
- Creates negative feedback loop

### Local Fatigue Issues
- Glycogen depletion
- Excitation-contraction coupling problems
- Diminishing returns per set

### Dose-Response Ceiling
- Anabolic pathways have capacity limits
- Extra volume past ceiling = damage without extra growth

### SFR Plummets
- More damage than can be healed
- Body prioritizes healing over growth

## Problems with High Frequency

### Lost Potentiation Effects
- Post-activation potentiation (sets 2+ better than set 1)
- Technique refinement across sets
- Mind-muscle connection improves across sets
- Metabolite/pump accumulation requires multiple sets

### Excessive Warm-ups
- More sessions = more time warming up
- Worse work:warmup ratio

### Connective Tissue Stress
- Joints/tendons recover slower than muscle
- High frequency accumulates CT damage
- More total reps = more joint wear

### Systemic MRV
- Individual muscle MRVs may be fine
- Total weekly volume can exceed systemic MRV

## Factors Affecting Recovery Time

### Recover Slower (lower frequency)
- Larger muscles
- Stronger muscles
- Higher fast-twitch percentage
- More stretch under tension (hams, triceps, pecs)
- Male sex (on average)
- Unified fiber architecture (pecs, lats, quads)

### Recover Faster (higher frequency)
- Smaller muscles
- Weaker muscles
- Higher slow-twitch percentage
- Less stretch under tension (delts, bis, forearms)
- Female sex (on average)
- Multi-directional fiber architecture (delts, glutes, traps)

## Research Summary
1. 1x/week insufficient vs 2x+
2. 3x slightly better than 2x; 4x barely better than 3x; 5x ≈ 4x
3. Higher frequencies support higher MRVs
4. Per-session MAV: ~5-10 sets typical; cap ~15 sets
5. Smaller muscles benefit more from high frequency

## Practical Guidelines

### Starting Point
- 2-4 sessions/muscle/week
- 3-8 sets/muscle/session average
- ~5-10 sets/session MAV range

### Session Volume Caps
- Per-session MRV: ~12 sets/muscle (cap ~15)
- Per-session total: ~25-30 sets max

### Frequency Deriving Algorithm
1. Train muscle at MEV
2. Wait until no longer sore
3. Train again; check performance
4. If performance maintained: keep interval
5. If performance drops: add 1 recovery day
6. After 2-5 sessions: stable frequency emerges

### Simpler Algorithm
1. Start 2x/week per muscle
2. Progress to ~10 sets/session
3. Note: soreness timing, performance
4. If healed early + good performance → add session next meso
5. If healed just in time → keep frequency
6. If overlapping soreness/performance loss → drop session

## Common Mistakes

### Under-Application
- Once/week for small muscles (biceps, side delts)
- Ignoring overlapping soreness/performance drops

### Over-Application
- Perfect symmetry obsession (7-day week is fine)
- High frequency year-round (need low freq mesos for CT recovery)
- Ignoring supporting muscle overuse

## Key Numbers
- Growth duration: 1-3 days post-training
- Optimal frequency: 2-4x/week/muscle
- Per-session: 3-8 sets average, cap ~12-15
- Total session: cap ~25-30 sets
- Female adjustment: +1 session/week vs male baseline
