# Glossary
Book: Scientific Principles of Hypertrophy Training | Authors: Dr. Mike Israetel, Dr. James Hoffmann, Dr. Melissa Davis, Jared Feather

## Training Structure Terms
- **Microcycle**: Usually 1 week. Repeating unit of sessions + rest
- **Mesocycle**: Multiple microcycles. Has accumulation phase → deload
- **Block**: 1+ mesocycles sharing a goal (muscle gain, fat loss)
- **Macrocycle**: Sequence of blocks; 1-4 blocks typical

## Phases
- **Accumulation**: Progressive overload phase. Increase sets/reps/load/effort weekly
- **Deload**: Reduced volume/load/effort. Dissipate fatigue, keep gains
- **Resensitization**: Near-MV training to reduce adaptive resistance

## Volume Landmarks
- **MV** (Maintenance Volume): Minimum to prevent muscle loss
- **MEV** (Minimum Effective Volume): Threshold for growth stimulus
- **MAV** (Maximum Adaptive Volume): Sweet spot for best gains/time
- **MRV** (Maximum Recoverable Volume): Upper limit before overreaching

## Effort Metrics
- **RIR** (Reps in Reserve): Reps left before failure. RIR 2 = stop with 2 left
- **Load**: Absolute weight used
- **Relative Load**: % of 1RM
- **Relative Effort**: How hard vs max; measured via RIR in hypertrophy

## Fatigue States
- **Fatigue**: Local (muscle-specific) + systemic (whole body)
- **Functional Overreaching**: Planned push at mesocycle end, followed by deload
- **Non-functional Overreaching**: Unplanned; disrupts training

## Diet Phases
- **Massing**: Hypercaloric + hypertrophy training for muscle gain
- **Cutting**: Hypocaloric + hypertrophy training for fat loss + muscle retention
- **Maintenance**: Eucaloric; often paired with resensitization
- **Minicut**: Short aggressive cut to potentiate next mass phase
